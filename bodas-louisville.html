What's included in a Jeca Audio wedding

A wedding is not one event, it's five: the ceremony, the cocktail hour, the grand entrance, the dinner and the party. We plan the sound and the timeline for all of them so nothing feels improvised.

  • Ceremony soundClean audio for vows and readings, plus a discreet speaker setup and a lapel or handheld mic for the officiant.
  • Bilingual MCGrand entrance, first dance, toasts, cake, bouquet and dollar dance announced clearly in English, Spanish, or both.
  • Custom playlistWe build the night around your must-play list and your do-not-play list, and read the room live to keep the floor full.
  • Dance-floor lightingLED bars, moving heads and beams sized to your venue, plus fog for the effect on the first dance if you want it.
  • Wireless microphonesFor the officiant, the toasts and the speeches — no passing a cable around the room.
  • Backup equipmentSpare speakers, mics, cables and a second controller travel with us to every wedding. The music does not stop.

Bilingual weddings are what we do best

Louisville is full of families where one side speaks English and the other speaks Spanish, and a wedding is exactly the moment where nobody should feel left out. We announce in both languages, we mix cumbia, banda and bachata alongside country, hip-hop and top 40, and we read which crowd is on the floor at any given moment. That's the difference between a room where people watch and a room where everyone dances. If you also want to keep costs down, we can quote a DJ-only package and rent you extra speakers or lighting separately.

Wedding DJ questions

How much does a wedding DJ cost in Louisville?

Jeca Audio wedding packages start at $499 for smaller receptions and go up to around $1,999 for full production with truss, moving heads and special effects. The final price depends on guest count, how many hours you need, whether we cover the ceremony as well as the reception, and how far the venue is. Send us your date and we'll give you an exact number with no obligation.

Do you cover the ceremony as well as the reception?

Yes. We can set up a separate sound system for the ceremony with microphones for the officiant and the readings, then move to the reception setup during the cocktail hour. Just tell us if the ceremony and reception are in the same venue or different ones so we can plan the timeline.

How far in advance should we book a wedding DJ?

Most couples book six to twelve months ahead, and Saturdays between April and October go first. If your date is sooner than that, message us anyway — we regularly have openings and can confirm the same day.

Can you MC in Spanish and English?

Yes, that's one of the main reasons couples hire us. Introductions, the grand entrance, toasts and every announcement can be delivered in both languages so both families follow along.

Do you take song requests from guests?

We do, within the limits you set. You give us a must-play list and a do-not-play list beforehand, and we filter guest requests against it so nothing you hate ends up on the speakers.

What happens if equipment fails during the wedding?

We bring backup speakers, microphones, cables and a spare controller to every wedding, and we arrive early enough to test everything before the first guest arrives. A failure means we swap gear, not that the music stops.

Do you offer uplighting and fog for the first dance?

Yes. LED uplighting, dance-floor lighting, standard fog and low-lying fog for the cloud effect on the first dance are all available. Cold sparks and confetti can be added too, subject to what your venue allows.

How do we reserve our date?

Message us on WhatsApp with your date and venue. We confirm availability, send the quote, and your date is held once you confirm and leave a deposit. The balance is due on the day of the wedding.
