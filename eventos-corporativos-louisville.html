Corporate event questions

How much does corporate event audio cost in Louisville?

It depends on room size, guest count, hours and whether you need a DJ or just a PA with microphones. Full packages start at $499; a straightforward speaker-and-microphone rental for a meeting costs considerably less. Send us the details and we'll quote the smallest setup that actually does the job.

Can you provide microphones for presentations and awards?

Yes. Wireless handheld and lapel microphones are available, along with spares, and we do a full sound check before the room opens so levels are set for the person actually speaking.

Can you play audio from our laptop or run our video?

Yes. We take a direct feed from a laptop so presentation videos play through the main system at the correct level, rather than through a laptop speaker nobody can hear.

Do you offer equipment rental without an operator?

Yes. Speakers, subwoofers, mixers, microphones, lighting and truss are all available to rent by the day, with pickup or delivery in the Louisville area. For simple meetings this is usually the most economical option.

How early do you set up?

We normally arrive 1.5 to 3 hours before the event depending on the package. If your venue requires everything in place earlier than that, early setup can be added to the quote.

Do you carry backup equipment?

Always. Spare speakers, microphones and cables travel to every event, so a hardware failure means a quick swap rather than a stopped program.

Do you serve venues outside Louisville?

Yes, up to roughly 60 miles, including Jeffersonville and New Albany IN, Shelbyville, Shepherdsville, Bardstown and Elizabethtown. Farther venues are possible — just ask.

How do we book and pay?

Message us on WhatsApp or call with the date, venue and headcount. We confirm availability and send the quote; the date is held with a deposit and the balance is due on the day of the event.
