Quinceañera DJ questions

How much does a DJ for a quinceañera cost in Louisville?

Packages start at $499 and go up to around $1,999 depending on guest count, hours, and how much lighting and special effects you want. Setup, breakdown, a bilingual MC and backup equipment are included in every package. Send us your date for an exact quote.

Do you coordinate the vals and the surprise dance?

Yes. We go over the order of the night with the family beforehand, cue the waltz for the chambelanes, and start the surprise dance track exactly as it was rehearsed, then cut cleanly into the open dance.

Can you announce everything in Spanish?

Yes. The entire program can be in Spanish, in English, or alternating between both. Most families in Louisville choose both so every guest follows along.

What music do you play at a quinceañera?

Whatever fills the floor: banda, cumbia, norteñas, bachata and merengue for the family, plus reggaetón, hip-hop and top 40 for her friends. You give us the must-play and do-not-play lists and we handle the mix live.

Do you offer cold sparks, fog and confetti?

Yes. Cold spark machines, standard fog, low-lying fog for the cloud effect during the vals, and electric or air-powered confetti launchers are all available as add-ons. Check with your venue first, since some halls restrict effects.

How long do you stay?

Packages are quoted by the block of hours you need, and extra hours can be added. Tell us your start time and roughly when you expect the venue to close and we'll quote the right length.

Do you need anything from the venue?

A standard power outlet near where we set up, and clear space for the speakers and lighting. Tell us if there are stairs or an elevator and whether the party is indoors or outdoors so we bring the right setup.

How do I reserve the date?

Message us on WhatsApp with the date and the venue. We confirm availability and send the quote; the date is held once you confirm and leave a deposit, with the balance due the day of the event.
